AIRBIOX

Coupling the atmospheric interactions with biological effects via a new chemical mechanism model targeting the formation of reactive oxygen species.

H.F.R.I. Project No. 29166. This project aims to connect chemical interactions in the atmosphere to mechanisms governing reactive oxygen species production and biological responses to exposure.
