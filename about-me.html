Dr. Eleni Dovrou

Assistant Professor · Department of Chemistry · University of Crete

Dr. Eleni Dovrou studies atmospheric chemical mechanisms and the links between air pollution and human health. Her work combines laboratory investigations, observations and modeling to understand how reactive gases, particles and environmental extremes influence atmospheric composition and biological responses.

She also holds an honorary visiting research affiliation with the University of Canberra's HEAL research community in Australia.

Her research encompasses wildfire plume chemistry, reactive oxygen species and oxidative stress in the lung, multiphase sulfate chemistry, aerosol formation, and indoor air quality.
