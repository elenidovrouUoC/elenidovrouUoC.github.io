Dr. Eleni Dovrou

Assistant Professor of Atmospheric Chemistry · Department of Chemistry, University of Crete

I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of Chemistry at the University of Crete and hold an honorary visiting research affiliation with the University of Canberra's HEAL Global Research Centre in Australia.

My research lies at the intersection of atmospheric chemistry and health effects. I examine chemical mechanisms associated with extreme events, their effects on air quality and human health, and reactions taking place within the human organism after exposure to atmospheric pollutants.

My work also explores molecular pathways that contribute to sulfate and organosulfate formation in the troposphere. I use laboratory measurements, atmospheric observations, and numerical modeling to understand these processes.

Before my appointment at the University of Crete, I worked with the Voulgarakis Group at the Technical University of Crete and Imperial College London, the Pandis Group at FORTH, the Pöschl and Berkemeier groups at the Max Planck Institute for Chemistry, and the Keutsch Group at Harvard University.

My doctoral research investigated previously underrepresented pathways involving reactive organic carbon and the formation of particulate sulfate and carbonaceous material in the atmosphere.
