Principal Investigator

Dr. Eleni Dovrou

Assistant Professor · Department of Chemistry · University of Crete

Dr. Eleni Dovrou leads the E-ACHE Lab. Her research focuses on atmospheric chemistry, wildfire-plume processes, reactive oxygen species, air quality, and links between atmospheric pollutants and human health.
