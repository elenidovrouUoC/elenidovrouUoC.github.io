OUTSIDE THE LAB

Non-scientific Activities

Interests and experiences beyond scientific research.

01
SPORT

Volleyball and beach volleyball

Volleyball has been an important part of my life since childhood. I have played competitively for clubs in Chania and Souda, and later for the Harvard Women's Volleyball Club. Team sports have taught me the value of collaboration, discipline and continual improvement.

I also competed in beach volleyball, including at national youth level in Greece and in tournaments in the United States.

◎
02
CREATIVITY

Music

I enjoy playing piano and exploring classical and contemporary music. Music provides another way to express ideas and creativity outside the laboratory.

03
EXPLORATION

Travel

Through research, sports and personal exploration, I have had opportunities to experience many places and cultures. Traveling has broadened my perspective, both scientifically and personally.
